About

I spent eight years running hotels — holding room inventory against revenue targets, closing the books before open, and fixing things in front of the person they went wrong for.

That work is not a detour from what I am doing now, it is the reason I can do it. An online store is inventory, margin, and logistics under a deadline, which is the job I have already been doing, only with the consequences arriving faster. I am putting that behind three things: building software, learning to value businesses properly, and getting a direct-to-customer venture off the ground.

None of them have arrived yet. I would rather say that plainly than dress up a plan as a track record.
