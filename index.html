Behind the desk

An online store is inventory, margin and logistics under a deadline. That is the job I have already been doing — only with the consequences arriving faster.

Hotels taught me the parts nobody photographs: what it costs when the count is wrong, how to close a night so the numbers survive daylight, and how to stand in front of the person something went wrong for. I have a Bachelor of Information Technology, so the building side is not new either — it just took me a while to point the two at the same thing.

None of the three doors have opened yet. I would rather say so plainly than dress a plan up as a track record.
