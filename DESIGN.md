# Design System: In Transit

## Overview

The surface is a split-flap departure board. That is not a decorative metaphor — it is the
information architecture. Every item on the page carries a status, and status is the grammar the
whole system is built from: `DEPARTED` for a closed chapter, `IN DEVELOPMENT` for work under way,
`SCHEDULED` for a plan not yet started. A portfolio whose subject has more direction than shipped
output would normally have to apologise for that gap; here the board's own vocabulary states it
plainly and the honesty reads as composure.

The physical model is a concourse, not a control room. The page ground is bone — warm, papery,
readable on a phone in daylight — and the dark graphite boards sit *on* it as objects, with the
shadow and the seam to prove they are objects. Light and dark are therefore not a theme toggle:
they are two different materials with fixed jobs. Bone is the room. Graphite is the board.

Type is mechanical rather than editorial. Archivo carries the display and body voice; Martian Mono
carries every piece of data — times, dates, statuses, column headers, captions — because on a real
board those are machine output, not prose. Mono is used for measurement, never as a costume for
"technical".

## Colors

Full-palette strategy with named roles, and the roles are semantic before they are decorative.

| Token | Role |
|---|---|
| `bone` / `bone-2` / `bone-3` | Concourse ground, alternate section ground, rules |
| `board` / `board-2` / `board-3` | Board object, recessed panel, raised flap face |
| `ink` / `ink-2` | Primary and secondary text on bone |
| `on-board` / `on-board-2` | Primary and secondary text on graphite |
| `amber` | Live / now / in-development status, and the primary action |
| `petrol` / `petrol-hi` | Scheduled and studying status |
| `error` | Form validation only |

**The two-ground rule.** Amber has two values and they are not interchangeable. `amber` (#D2891F)
reaches 6.06:1 on graphite and is the only one permitted there. On bone it drops to 2.3:1 and fails,
so bone-ground amber text uses `amber-ink` (#85510A) at 5.39:1. Petrol inverts the same way:
`petrol` on bone, `petrol-hi` on graphite. Any new component that can appear on both grounds must
declare both values.

**Focus follows the surface.** A `--focus` custom property is set to `amber-ink` at `:root` and
overridden to `amber-hi` on every dark region (`.rail`, `.hero__board`, `.sect--build`,
`.sect--contact`, `.foot`), so the focus ring never disappears into its own background.

## Typography

Two families, five roles, and a hard split between prose and data.

- **display** — the name, at the board's own scale. Capped at 5rem; tracking −0.03em.
- **heading** — section headings, same mechanical weight, one step down.
- **body** — 1rem/1.6, measure held to 60–65ch.
- **data** — Martian Mono, 0.625–0.75rem, letter-spacing 0.06–0.18em, usually uppercase. Times,
  dates, employers, statuses, column headers, captions. If it would be printed by a machine, it is
  mono.
- **hanzi** — Noto Sans SC, subset to the ten characters the page actually uses, for the widget and
  the 雅 in the project title.

The 65–75ch measure applies to prose only; data lines are short by nature and are not padded to
reach it.

## Layout

Content width is `min(100% - 2.5rem, 76rem)`, widening the gutter to 5rem at ≥48em. Spacing runs on
a 4px base through `--s1`–`--s10`, with more space above a heading than below it.

Breakpoints, all min-width: **34em** (status rows and the stack key gain columns), **40em** (rail
navigation appears), **44em** / **68em** (credentials go 2-up then 4-up), **48em** (wider gutter),
**52em** (the departures board becomes a real three-column table and its column headers appear),
**56em** (record rows and the contact split), **60em** (hero becomes two columns), **62em** (the
build section's sticky demo column).

**Column headers earn their existence.** `.board__head` is `display: none` below 52em. Labels for
columns that have collapsed into a single stack are noise, not structure.

**The portrait must not set the hero's height.** At ≥60em the hero's `<picture>` is
`flex: 1 1 0; min-height: 0` with the image absolutely positioned inside it, so the copy column
governs the board's height. Left to its intrinsic 1:2 aspect the portrait drags the hero past the
fold.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is physical and sparing — three shadows, all with offset and blur, no zero-offset halos.

- Board panel: `0 24px 60px -28px rgba(20,16,12,.55), 0 2px 0 #000`
- Flap cell: `0 2px 5px rgba(0,0,0,.45)`
- Phone: `0 30px 60px -24px rgba(0,0,0,.75)`, with `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.06)` as the top edge highlight

The flap cell's face is a three-stop gradient with a 2px dark band at the vertical midpoint. That
band is the seam of the physical flap and is what makes the tile read as hardware rather than as a
rounded rectangle.

## Shapes

Radii are small and mechanical: 2px on flaps, chips and inputs; 3px on board panels. The soft radii
belong only to the objects that are soft in life — the widget (0.9rem) and the phone shell
(1.9rem). Borders are 1px throughout: `--rule` on bone, `--rule-board` (#33363B) on graphite.
Chips are outlined in `currentColor`, never filled.

Icons are authored SVG on a 24×24 box, `stroke-width: 1.75`, square caps and miter joins to match
the mechanical register. No glyph or emoji icons.

## Components

**Split-flap text** (`.flap`) — the system's signature. Text is split into per-character cells that
churn through a charset and settle, staggered. Three charsets: uppercase Latin, lowercase Latin with
pinyin diacritics, and the hanzi subset; the charset is chosen from the target string's own
character class so a lowercase word never churns through capitals. Two variants: plain, and
`data-tiles` which adds the flap-cell chrome and a per-step `scaleY` flip. Accessibility is
structural, not optional — the container carries `aria-label` with the whole word and `role="text"`,
and every cell is `aria-hidden`, or a screen reader spells the name out letter by letter.

**Status chip** (`.chip`) — the status vocabulary. `chip--live` blinks its dot on a 2.4s cycle,
because a board blinks what is imminent; the blink is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Board row** (`.board__row`) — a three-column data row: destination at display scale, detail as
prose, status right-aligned. Hover tints the row and shifts the destination to `amber-ink`.

**Record row** (`.record__row`) — date in mono, role and employer stacked, one concrete operating
fact, status chip. Ten of these carry the career section.

**Widget** (`.widget`) — a bone card on the phone's lock screen cycling hanzi, pinyin and English
through the flap engine. It is the product's mechanism shown working, not a screenshot of it.

## Do's and Don'ts

- **Do** give every new item a status. An item with no status does not belong on this board.
- **Do** pick the amber and petrol value from the ground the element sits on, and declare both when
  the component can appear on either.
- **Do** use mono for machine output — times, dates, statuses, labels, captions — and Archivo for
  everything a person wrote.
- **Do** gate every animation behind `prefers-reduced-motion`, and keep text visible by default so
  content never depends on a reveal firing.
- **Don't** let an image's intrinsic aspect set a section's height.
- **Don't** show column headers for columns that have collapsed at that breakpoint.
- **Don't** state anything on this page that PRODUCT.md does not record as confirmed. The board's
  whole credibility rests on its statuses being true.
- **Don't** add card grids. This system's container is the ruled row, not the card; nested cards
  are always wrong here.
